How to use

  1. Apply a small amount to clean, dry feet.
  2. Massage into heels, soles and other rough areas.
  3. Allow the balm to absorb; apply before bed for a longer conditioning treatment.

The experience

Your end-of-day reset

Peppermint and eucalyptus provide a crisp, cooling sensation while chamomile rounds out the aroma with a softer herbal note.

Good to know: For external use only. Patch test before first use. Avoid contact with eyes and discontinue use if irritation occurs. Store closed in a cool, dry place.
